Upcoming earnings, and what the market has already decided about them. Generated 2026-08-21 10:01 · universe 3049 names · snapshot history 40,911 rows
Not investment advice, and the direction column is not a signal. Fifteen directional strategies were backtested over 83,366 prints from 2016–2026 against a random-entry control. None survived train, validate and holdout. The best (buy a >5% beat, hold 20 days) cleared the first two periods and then failed out of sample. So the Stance column is a transparent summary of the panels, not an edge.

What did hold up across all three periods is magnitude: stocks in the quietest quartile of past earnings reactions went on to move 0.54× as much as a random name (0.54 / 0.58 / 0.54 across train / validate / holdout), and the loudest quartile moved 1.56× as much (1.55 / 1.47 / 1.56). How to read this. Implied is the ATM straddle on the first expiry after the print, as a percentage of spot — what options are charging for the event. Realised is the median absolute one-day move over the last eight prints. A ratio well above 1 means expectations are expensive; well below 1 means cheap. P(beat) is the historical frequency with which similar-looking names beat, fitted on data through 2020-12-31 only. Run-up 10d is how much is already in the price going in.

Revision data comes from this project's own daily consensus snapshots, seeded with Yahoo's 90-day lookback. Coverage deepens every day the collector runs.
